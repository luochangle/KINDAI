Free medical AI for elders

Care that reaches every elder, everywhere.

KindAI builds free, non-invasive screening and support tools that work on any phone — so early detection and daily help don't depend on where an elder lives or what they can pay.

See the apps → Why we built this

We believe medical AI tools should not be locked behind corporate walls — and especially shouldn't depend on where an elder lives or what an elder can pay.

All our apps are free for elders. Forever.

What we're building

A growing family of tools, all voice-first.

Live · Android

VoicePD

Non-invasive Parkinson's screening through acoustic voice analysis. An elder simply speaks into their phone and receives a preliminary screening in seconds. Presented at IEEE ICHI 2026.

In development · Android & iOS

Voice memory companion

Helps elders record their memories by voice, with gentle emotion sensing and spoken reminders — inspired by watching elders reach for a simple voice recorder.
